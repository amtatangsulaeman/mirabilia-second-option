Visiting a centre in person is the best way to know if it feels right for your child. A good tour should leave you with clear answers, not more questions. Here is a simple checklist to take with you.

## About the educators

1. **How long have the educators in my child's room been at the centre?** Familiar faces help children feel secure.
2. **What qualifications does the team have?** Ask about early childhood degrees, diplomas and first aid training.
3. **How do you help new children settle in?** Look for a flexible, gentle approach. Read our [settling-in tips](/blog/settling-into-childcare).

## About the day

4. **What does a typical day look like?** Ask about play, rest, meals and outdoor time.
5. **How much time do children spend outside?**
6. **What meals and snacks are provided?** Check how allergies and dietary needs are managed.
7. **How are sleep and rest times handled?** Especially important for babies and toddlers.

## About learning

8. **How do you plan learning for each child?** Centres should follow the Early Years Learning Framework. Here is [how our curriculum works](/our-curriculum).
9. **Are there extra programs?** For example music, cooking or sport. See our [Squad Programs](/squad-programs).

## About communication

10. **How will I know how my child's day went?** Many centres use an app such as Storypark for photos and updates.
11. **Who do I talk to if I have a concern?**

## About fees

12. **What are the daily fees, and what is included?** Ask how the [Child Care Subsidy](/childcare-subsidy) will reduce what you pay, and whether nappies, meals and programs are included.

## Trust your feelings

Notice how the room feels. Are the children busy and happy? Do educators get down to the children's level and speak kindly? Your instinct matters.
