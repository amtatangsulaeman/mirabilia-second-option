Starting childcare is a big change for children and parents alike. Some little ones walk straight in and start playing, while others need a few weeks to feel at home. Both are completely normal. These tips from our educators can make the first weeks easier for everyone.

## Before the first day

### 1. Visit together first

Come in for a tour or a short stay-and-play so your child can see the room, the toys and the outdoor area with you by their side. Familiar places feel safer. You can [book a tour](/book-a-tour) at any of our centres.

### 2. Talk about it in a positive way

In the days before, talk about what they will do: painting, the sandpit, lunch with friends. Picture books about starting childcare can help too. Keep it light and cheerful.

### 3. Start with shorter days if you can

A few shorter days in the first week help your child get used to the new routine without getting too tired.

## On the day

### 4. Pack something from home

A favourite soft toy, a family photo or a blanket that smells like home can be a real comfort, especially at rest time.

### 5. Keep goodbyes short and loving

Give a cuddle, say a clear goodbye and tell them when you will be back ("after afternoon tea"). Long goodbyes, or slipping out without saying goodbye, usually make it harder.

> "A short, confident goodbye tells your child that this is a safe place. We will always call you if they need you." — Mirabilia educator

### 6. Trust the educators

It is normal for children to cry at drop-off. Most settle within minutes once you have gone, and our educators will comfort them and help them join in.

## In the first few weeks

### 7. Stay in touch

We share photos and updates through **Storypark**, so you can see how your child's day is going. You are always welcome to call the centre too.

### 8. Be patient with yourself and your child

Some children settle in a week, others take a month. Tiredness, clinginess or big feelings at home are common in the early weeks and usually pass as the new routine becomes familiar.
