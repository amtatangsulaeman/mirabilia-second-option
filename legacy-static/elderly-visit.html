Connecting Generations with Love and Care

01

Introduction

At Mirabilia, we believe in fostering empathy, kindness, and community spirit from a young age. Our Elderly Visit Squad Program is a unique initiative designed to bridge the generational gap by bringing children and seniors together. Through regular visits to local aged care homes, our children have the opportunity to connect with the elderly, learning valuable life lessons and spreading joy and companionship.

02

About the Program

Our Elderly Visit Program is carefully structured to ensure meaningful and positive interactions between children and seniors. The visits typically include activities such as reading stories, playing games, singing songs, and simply spending time together. These experiences offer both children and the elderly an opportunity to form friendships, share stories, and create lasting memories

03

Benefits for Children

Participating in the Elderly Visit Program helps children develop empathy, compassion, and a sense of community. By interacting with seniors, children learn about different life experiences, history, and the importance of kindness and respect. These visits also provide children with an understanding of ageing and the valuable role seniors play in society.

04

Benefits for the Elderly

For the elderly, the presence of children brings a sense of joy, vitality, and companionship. Many seniors look forward to these visits as they provide an opportunity for social interaction, emotional support, and a break from the routine. The laughter, energy, and curiosity of children often brighten the days of the elderly, creating a mutually beneficial relationship between the two generations.

Why Mirabilia

Why Participate in the Elderly Visit Program?

Building Empathy

Children learn the importance of kindness, respect, and understanding by spending time with the elderly

Creating Connections

The program helps foster meaningful relationships and builds a sense of community between the young and old

Emotional and Social Growth

Through interaction with the elderly, children develop emotional intelligence and enhance their social skills

Fun and Engaging Activities

Each visit is filled with enjoyable activities that both the children and the elderly can participate in together

Join us!

Help your child become a part of something special by enrolling them in our Elderly Visit Program at Mirabilia Centre. Together, we can build a compassionate and connected community, one visit at a time.

Acknowledgement of Country

Mirabilia acknowledges the traditional owners of all lands across Australia. We acknowledge and pay our respects to the Elders of the past, present and emerging. We thank you for sharing and caring for the land we learn and play on.
