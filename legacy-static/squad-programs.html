squad programs

Mirabilia’s Squad Programs offer a diverse range of enriching activities designed to inspire, educate, and engage children. From cooking and music to sports and community involvement, each program nurtures essential skills, creativity, and social development in a fun, supportive environment.

Squad Programs

What is a Squad Program?

Our class is designed to promote physical activity and improve coordination.

Through lively dance routines, rhythmic exercises, and movement games, children enhance their motor skills and balance while having a blast. These activities support their overall physical development and help them become more aware of their bodies in space. Additionally, group activities encourage social interaction, teamwork, and communication, helping children build strong social skills and make new friends.

01

Cooking Class

We understand that early learning goes beyond academics—it's also about fostering creativity, independence, and a love for new experiences. Our Cooking Classes are designed to provide a fun, educational, and hands-on culinary experience for young learners. With a focus on creativity, safety, and skill-building, our classes make cooking an engaging activity that kids will look forward to.

Our cooking classes are a wonderful opportunity for children to interact with their peers in a collaborative setting. Through group activities, teamwork, and sharing, kids learn important social skills and build friendships while enjoying the cooking process.

02

Music Class

Our Music Classes are designed to nurture your child’s love for music while enhancing their cognitive, social, and emotional skills. Through engaging activities, playful learning, and musical exploration, we provide a rich and stimulating environment where young learners can thrive.

Through group activities and collaborative musical projects, children learn to work together, share, and communicate. Our classes provide a supportive environment where kids can develop social skills, empathy, and emotional intelligence while enjoying the joy of making music with others.

Learn More
03

Sports Class

At Mirabilia Early Learning Education Byford Centre, our Sports Class is designed to keep young children active and engaged while fostering their physical development. Through fun and age-appropriate activities, we encourage children to build coordination, strength, and teamwork skills.

Our passionate educators create a supportive environment where children can explore various sports and movement exercises, helping them develop a love for physical activity. By integrating play with structured sports activities, we ensure that each child enjoys the benefits of exercise while learning important life skills like cooperation and perseverance.

Elderly Visit

Our program provides young children with the opportunity to interact with elderly individuals, gaining valuable life lessons, storytelling experiences, and a greater appreciation for different generations. These interactions help children develop empathy, respect, and social skills.

We believe in the power of connection. Our visits are designed to be heartwarming and enjoyable for both children and elderly visitors. Through activities like reading stories, sharing crafts, or simply talking, we create joyful moments and lasting memories.

Acknowledgement of Country

Mirabilia acknowledges the traditional owners of all lands across Australia. We recognise and celebrate the contributions of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people as the First Australians, including their role in the education and care of children. We acknowledge and pay our respects to the Elders of the past, present and emerging. We thank you for sharing and caring for the land we learn and play on. We endeavour to continue to respect, share and care for this beautiful land.
