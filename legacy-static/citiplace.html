Our Educators

Our team of educators are committed and passionate about ensuring your child will be safe, nurtured and cared for every day.

At Mirabilia, children are encouraged to engage in interest-led play that creates learning and meaningful experiences.

Our educators seek to optimise diverse learning opportunities and implement the Curriculum as framed by the Early Years Learning Framework.

You and Your Child

Mirabilia Early Learning Education Centres provide a Centre-to-Family communication platform so families can maintain contact with their children’s experiences through photos, stories and learning outcomes.

Our Centres use Storypark and each family is provided with a secure login to access on their computers, mobiles or apps.

Cooking Class

We understand that early learning goes beyond academics—it's also about fostering creativity, independence, and a love for new experiences. Our Cooking Classes are designed to provide a fun, educational, and hands-on culinary experience for young learners. With a focus on creativity, safety, and skill-building, our classes make cooking an engaging activity that kids will look forward to.

Our cooking classes are a wonderful opportunity for children to interact with their peers in a collaborative setting. Through group activities, teamwork, and sharing, kids learn important social skills and build friendships while enjoying the cooking process.

Meet the team
“I have developed an interest in Early Childhood as I come from a large family in Ireland. As the oldest sibling, I have developed a nurturing approach towards children. As the director of Mirabilia Citiplace it is my mission to ensure each and every child in our care has the most positive, happy experience of growth through learning and playing in each of the days they spend here with us.

I aim to provide a safe, positive and nurturing environment for children to explore, discover, create and imagine. My goal as the centre director is to create ‘A home away from home’ for children, staff and families at Mirabilia. I believe that children are unique individuals, it is important in their younger years for them to have a sense of belonging in their care place.

I thrive in helping my staff, supporting them and empowering them. “Together we can do so much”.

Abbie WrayCentre Director at Mirabilia Citiplace

Acknowledgement of Country

Mirabilia acknowledges the traditional owners of all lands across Australia. We recognise and celebrate the contributions of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people as the First Australians, including their role in the education and care of children. We acknowledge and pay our respects to the Elders of the past, present and emerging. We thank you for sharing and caring for the land we learn and play on. We endeavour to continue to respect, share and care for this beautiful land.
