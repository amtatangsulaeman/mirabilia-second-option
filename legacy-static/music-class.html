Squad Programs

Our Music Class at Mirabilia Childcare Centre is an enchanting journey into the world of rhythm, melody, and sound. Designed for children of all ages, this program introduces the basics of music in a playful and engaging manner.

We believe music is a universal language that inspires creativity, fosters emotional expression, and brings joy to every child's heart.

02

What Makes Our Music Class Unique?

Music is more than just entertainment — it’s a tool for holistic development. Our Music Class nurtures a child’s innate sense of rhythm and musicality, providing opportunities to sing, dance, play instruments, and explore the wonderful world of sound. Each session is tailored to different age groups and developmental stages, ensuring an inclusive environment where every child can participate and thrive.

03

Benefits of Music Education

Research shows that music education has numerous benefits for children, including enhanced cognitive development, improved memory, and increased social and emotional skills. In our Music Class, children learn to appreciate different genres of music, experiment with various instruments, and understand fundamental musical concepts such as beat, tempo, and pitch. This fun and interactive program also promotes teamwork, cooperation, and social interaction.

04

Interactive and Fun Sessions

Our Music Class is designed to be lively, interactive, and above all, fun! We incorporate singing, dancing, and instrument play to keep children engaged and excited. From playing the tambourine to creating rhythms with maracas, every session is a new adventure in sound. We also introduce children to simple music theory through games and activities, making learning enjoyable and easy to grasp.

Why Mirabilia

Why Enroll in Mirabilia’s Music Class?

Inclusive Environment

Our classes are open to all children, regardless of their musical background or abilities

Expert Educators

Our instructors are experienced music teachers who are passionate about nurturing young talent

Comprehensive Learning

Children are exposed to various aspects of music, from singing and playing instruments to understanding rhythm and melody

Encourages Emotional Expression

Music is a wonderful way for children to express their feelings and emotions in a safe and creative space

Join us!

Let your child explore the magical world of music with Mirabilia Childcare Centre’s Music Class. Enrol now and watch as they discover new sounds, make new friends, and develop a lifelong love for music!
