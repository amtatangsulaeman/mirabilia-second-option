About The Promotion

What Is The Promotion?

We’re offering 25% off childcare fees for all new enrolments at our Mirabilia Childcare Centres:

  • At our Citiplace location in Perth CBD, receive 25% off for 25 weeks.
  • Caversham, Byford & Champion Lakes locations, enjoy 25% off for 1 month.

This limited-time offer is perfect for parents looking for high-quality childcare with experienced educators in a safe, nurturing environment.

Secure your child’s place today!

Who Is Eligible for This Offer?

This limited 25% discount is available to new families who enrol their child at any Mirabilia Childcare Centre.

It’s perfect for parents looking for a reliable, nurturing, and high-quality childcare centre that supports children from 6 weeks to 5 years old—helping them thrive before starting preschool.

How Do I Claim This Offer?

Claiming your 25% discount at Mirabilia Childcare Centre is simple!

Fill out the form above – Our friendly team will contact you to guide you through the enrolment process and apply your discount.

Alternatively you can book a tour directly on our Booking Page and schedule a tour at any of our child care locations; Caversham, Byford, Citiplace or Champion Lakes. Mention the 25% offer, and we’ll handle the rest while you explore our nurturing environment.

Whether you're ready to enrol or want to see our facilities firsthand, we're here to make the process easy and stress-free for you and your child. Don’t miss out, spaces are limited.

Why Are We Offering This?

At Mirabilia Childcare Centre, we believe that every child deserves the best start in life.

This limited-time offer is our way of making it easier for families to experience the high-quality care and education we provide.

We understand that finding the right childcare can be overwhelming—as a parent you want a safe, nurturing environment for your child to learn, grow, and thrive.

That’s exactly what we offer across our childcare locations!

This offer allows you to experience the Mirabilia difference; a place where your child is supported by experienced educators, engaging learning programs, and a family-friendly atmosphere, while making childcare more accessible and affordable.

Take advantage of this offer and give your child the best possible start!
